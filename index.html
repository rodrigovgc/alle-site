Three ways to make a deck

2

With an AI prompt

Answer a few questions and Alle writes a prompt for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Paste the reply back and your deck is ready.

3

Questions

Is Alle free?

Yes. Alle is free to use. You bring your own Google Sheet or AI chat, so there's nothing to pay.

How do I create a deck?

Publish a Google Sheet as CSV with two columns, or use the built-in AI prompt builder and paste the result back. You can also start from a ready-made deck.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Alle runs in the browser. On iPhone you can add it to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience.

Can I use images?

Yes. A card can show an image, so you can study flags, road signs or any picture, and Alle can draw analog clocks for telling the time.

Where is my data?

Your account, decks and progress are stored securely. Your passwords are never visible to anyone, including us.
