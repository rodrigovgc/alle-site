Who we are

Alle is a learning app for making and studying flashcards. It is run by its founder, Rodrigo, as an independent project based in Brussels, Belgium. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the controller of the personal data described here.

In short: we only collect what we need to run Alle and answer your messages. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and the website only uses analytics cookies if you agree to them.

What we collect

When you create an account

Your email address, an encrypted version of your password, and basic account records such as when you signed up and last signed in.

What you add to Alle

Your decks and cards, the links to the Google Sheets you import, deck settings such as colours and names, and your study progress, such as which cards you got right and when they're due again.

When you send us feedback

The message you write, the topic you choose, and the page you sent it from. Your name, your email and any screenshots or files are optional, and we only receive them if you add them.

How you use the app

Simple events inside the app, such as adding a deck, finishing a study session, or choosing light or dark mode. They're stored with your account in our own database, never shared with analytics companies, and we only look at them as totals, to understand which features help and what to improve.

Technical information

Like any website, our hosting provider automatically receives technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run Alle. They process data only on our instructions.

  • Supabase: user accounts, the database for decks, progress and feedback, and storage for feedback attachments.
  • Vercel: hosting for the Alle website and app.
  • Resend: sending account emails, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.
  • Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd): website statistics, only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Hotjar (Hotjar Ltd, Malta): heatmaps and visit recordings on the website, only if you accept analytics cookies.

Some of these providers are based in, or may access data from, countries outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. When that happens, the transfer is protected by safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Our fonts are served from our own website, so loading a page doesn't send your data to a font provider.

Cookies and storage

The Alle website doesn't use advertising cookies. With your permission, it uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from, and Hotjar to see how pages are used, through heatmaps and recordings of clicks, taps and scrolling (with text you type hidden). Both set cookies and only run if you choose “Accept” in the cookie banner; if you choose “No thanks”, neither loads at all. IP addresses are shortened before storage. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the homepage. The Alle app itself doesn't use Google Analytics or Hotjar.

When you sign in to the app, your browser stores a sign-in token so you stay logged in, and your display preferences, such as light or dark mode, are kept on your device. These are strictly necessary for the app to work as you set it up, so they don't require consent.

AI and Google Sheets

AI prompts. Alle can write a prompt for you to use with an AI service of your choice. Alle doesn't send anything to that service. You copy the prompt yourself, and anything you share with an AI service is covered by that service's own privacy policy. Please don't put sensitive personal information into prompts.

Google Sheets. When you import a sheet, Alle reads the published CSV link you provide. Anyone with a published link can view that sheet, so only publish content you're comfortable sharing. Google's own privacy policy applies to your use of Google Sheets.

How long we keep it

  • Account, learning and usage data: for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account, then Delete account), which removes it straight away together with your decks, progress and usage events. If you ask us instead, we do it within 30 days, unless we're legally required to keep something.
  • Feedback and attachments: for up to 24 months, or sooner if you ask us to delete them.
  • Technical logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period, typically days to weeks, for security and troubleshooting.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete
  • have your data deleted, including your whole account
  • restrict or object to how we use your data
  • receive your data in a portable format

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We'll reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données) at dataprotectionauthority.be, or to the authority in the EU country where you live.

Children

Alle is not intended for children under 13. If you are under the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country (13 in Belgium), please use Alle only with the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data without that involvement, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Alle grows, for example when new features are added. We'll change the date at the top of this page, and for important changes we'll let you know in the app or by email.

Contact

For any question about your privacy or to use your rights, send us a message through the contact form on our homepage. Choose “Other” and tell us what you'd like us to do.
