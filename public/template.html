Google Sheets flashcard template

A ready-to-fill Google Sheet for making your own flashcards. One column for the front of each card, one for the answer. Make a copy, fill it in, and study it in Alle.

Make a copy in Google Sheets

Google opens a “Make a copy” screen and saves the copy in your own Google Drive, so you can edit it freely.

How to use it

  1. Make a copy with the button above.
  2. Rename the two columns in the first row, for example English and Dutch. They become the labels on your cards.
  3. Add one card per row: what you see in the first column, the answer in the second.
  4. Add it to Alle. See how to turn a Google Sheet into flashcards, including how to publish the sheet and paste its link.

Tips. To accept more than one right answer, separate them with a vertical bar: gaan|lopen. To show a picture, put an image link in the first column.
